Where a refund actually comes from

Purchases are made on Gumroad, which takes the payment and holds the money. So Gumroad is the party that issues a refund, and the refund policy shown on the product page at checkout is Gumroad's.

The 60-day guarantee below is a personal commitment on top of that, backed by the operator of this site. If Gumroad's own window has passed, the money is returned directly instead. Either way you are not left arguing with a form.

1. The short version

  • Workbooks: 60-day money-back guarantee, any reason. If it did not change a booking decision in two months, it did not do its job.
  • There are no subscriptions to cancel. Every product is a one-off purchase of a file. Nothing renews and nothing can auto-charge you.
  • EU and UK consumers: a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal, which the 60-day guarantee is longer than anyway.

Refunding somebody quickly beats arguing with them. A carrier who felt cheated tells twelve other carriers, and in this industry they all know each other.

2. EU and UK: your 14-day right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU or the UK, the Consumer Rights Directive gives you 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract without giving a reason.

For digital content and services, the 14 days run from the day the contract was concluded — the day you ordered — not from the day you downloaded anything.

This right belongs to consumers. A carrier buying as a registered business is generally not a consumer for this purpose, though our own guarantee in section 4 applies to everyone regardless.

3. Why instant download asks you to waive it

Article 16(m) of the Consumer Rights Directive lets a seller exclude the withdrawal right for digital content only where all of the following are true:

  1. You gave prior express consent for the download to begin immediately, inside the 14-day period;
  2. You acknowledged that doing so means you lose the right of withdrawal; and
  3. The seller gave you confirmation of that agreement on a durable medium.

At checkout you will be asked to tick a box reading substantially:

"I request immediate access to this digital product and I acknowledge that by doing so I lose my 14-day right of withdrawal once the download begins."

Two things worth saying plainly. First, a pre-ticked box does not count — the consent has to be an active choice, and if you find that box already ticked, the waiver is not valid. Second, waiving the statutory right does not waive our 60-day guarantee. That guarantee is ours to give and we do not withdraw it because you clicked download.

4. The 60-day guarantee

60 days, any reason, on every purchase.

Not "unused" — you cannot unsee a spreadsheet, and pretending otherwise would be theatre. Use it, put a month of real loads through it, and if it did not earn its price, ask for the money back.

You are not asked to delete your copy. If the workbook is genuinely useless to you, taking it away as well would be petty.

This guarantee is given personally by the operator of this site and is honoured whether or not Gumroad's own refund window is still open — see the note at the top of this page.

5. How to ask for a refund

Purchases are processed by Gumroad, so Gumroad issues the refund. Two ways to start it:

  1. Email aleksnvkvc@gmail.com with the order number from your receipt. It gets authorised with Gumroad. This is the fastest route and you do not need to explain yourself.
  2. Use Gumroad's own refund process, linked from your purchase receipt.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Gumroad typically processes within a few business days; your bank may take a few more. Neither of those steps is under this site's control, but say so if it stalls and it gets chased.

You may also withdraw by any clear statement — the EU model withdrawal form is one option, but an email saying "I want to withdraw from this purchase" is legally sufficient and we will treat it as such.

6. When a refund is refused

Short list, published here rather than kept as a surprise:

  • Repeated buy-and-refund of the same product. Once is a mistake; a pattern is something else.
  • Redistribution. If a product has been resold or published in breach of the licence, the refund is refused and the licence terminated.
  • Beyond 60 days, unless the product is faulty — see section 7, which has no time limit of that kind.

"I did not read what it does" is not on this list. That refund is granted.

7. If the product is faulty

Separate from any refund window: if a workbook has a broken formula, or the software does not do what this site says it does, that is a defect and you are entitled to have it fixed, replaced or refunded under Serbian consumer law and, for EU consumers, under Directive (EU) 2019/770 on digital content.

Report it to aleksnvkvc@gmail.com. If a calculation is wrong we want to know more than you want to tell us — the same arithmetic runs in four places in this product, and an error in one is an error worth hunting down in all of them.
