Refunds

How refunds work for a business that sells services only.

Version 2.2 Last updated 10 October 2026 Governing law: Republic of Serbia
  • 1. The short version
  • 2. Before work starts
  • 3. $49 Load Check promise
  • 4. After delivery
  • 5. Monthly services

1. The short version

  • Nothing is sold through this site, so there is no store refund. Every paid service is agreed and invoiced by email.
  • If I can't deliver what we agreed, you get your money back in full.
  • The $49 Load Check is money-back: if the report doesn't help you decide on that load, you get the $49 back.
  • If a report has a mistake in the arithmetic or uses a figure differently from what you sent, it is corrected at no cost.

2. Before work starts

You can cancel at any time before the work begins and any payment is returned in full. Payment is normally due before the report is delivered, so in practice you are never paying for something you haven't agreed to.

3. $49 Load Check promise

If a Quick Load Check report doesn't help you decide whether to take, counter or pass on that load, email me within 7 days of receiving it and the $49 is refunded. You don't need to argue the case; tell me why it didn't help, so I can improve the next one.

If you order the $249 Full Cost & Lane Audit within 30 days of a paid Load Check, the $49 is taken off the audit price. A Load Check that was refunded doesn't count toward the audit.

4. After delivery

An analysis is work done on your numbers, so once it is delivered it can't be "returned". But you are covered in two ways:

  • Errors: if the report contains a calculation error, or doesn't use the figures you sent, reply and it will be corrected free of charge.
  • Not what we agreed: if what you received is clearly not what was agreed in writing, and it can't be put right, the payment is refunded.

For services other than the $49 Load Check, a report that is correct but gives an answer you didn't want (for example, that a load doesn't pay) isn't a ground for a refund. That answer is the service.

5. Monthly services

Load Check Basic starts with a 7-day free trial with no payment taken, so there is nothing to refund if you stop during it. After the trial, Load Check Basic, the Load Check Plan and Ongoing Support are invoiced one month at a time. Stop whenever you like and nothing further is invoiced. If you stop during a month that is already paid and no work has been done for that month yet, that month is refunded.

6. EU and UK consumers

If you buy as a consumer in the EU or the UK, you have a 14-day right to withdraw from a service contract. If you ask for the work to start within those 14 days, you will be asked to confirm that by email, and once a service has been fully delivered with your agreement the right of withdrawal ends. Most customers here buy as a business, and the policy above applies to everyone either way.

7. How to ask

Email aleksnvkvc@gmail.com with the invoice number or the date you paid. Refunds go back the way you paid (PayPal or Payoneer), normally within 5 business days of being agreed.
