How the owner-operator take-home pay calculator works

Formula & FAQ +

This owner-operator take-home calculator estimates what you keep in a year after running the truck. It starts from your loaded miles and rate per mile, then subtracts fuel on all miles including deadhead, maintenance, truck and trailer payments, insurance and other fixed costs.

What is left is profit before tax. It then sets aside the tax percentage you enter and shows your take-home, an hourly figure at 60 hours a week for 48 weeks, and the break-even rate per loaded mile.

gross revenue = loaded miles × rate per mile
fuel = loaded miles × (1 + deadhead %) ÷ MPG × diesel price
take-home = gross − fuel − maintenance − payments − insurance − other fixed costs − tax set-aside

How much does an owner-operator take home?

It depends almost entirely on cost per mile and miles run. Put in your own numbers; averages hide the difference between a good and a bad year.

How much should I set aside for tax?

Many owner-operators set aside 25–30% of profit for self-employment and income tax. Check with an accountant for your situation.
