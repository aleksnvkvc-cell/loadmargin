Read this first: who you are dealing with

LoadMargin is run by one person, not a company. There is no registered legal entity behind it, and this page says so rather than implying otherwise through corporate language.

That has two concrete consequences, stated plainly rather than buried:

  • This website sells nothing and takes no payment. There is no checkout here, no card form, no account and no subscription. Every paid product is sold on Gumroad, under Gumroad's own terms, and your contract for a purchase is formed there.
  • The free tools are genuinely free and complete. The calculator, the seven calculators and the fleet console are the real thing, not a limited preview. They run in your browser and cost nothing.
