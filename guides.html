Why these exist

The math is not the hard part

Freight profitability is arithmetic a fourteen-year-old could do. What makes it hard is that nobody writes down the inputs, and the industry substitutes a shorthand — rate per mile — that quietly ignores the two costs deciding most loads.

These guides exist so the method is checkable. If a carrier's controller wants to know how a margin was derived, the answer should be a link, not a phone call.
