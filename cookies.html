Cookies & Local Storage

We set no cookies at all. Here is exactly what the application does store in your browser, and why that is not the same thing.

Version 1.0 Last updated 26 August 2026 Governing law: Republic of Serbia

1. The short version

This site sets no cookies. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no tracking, no consent banner — because there is nothing to consent to.

The fleet console stores your data in your browser's local storage, which is not a cookie and is never transmitted anywhere. That is the whole story, and the rest of this page is the detail behind it.

2. What is actually stored

NameTypeWhat it holdsLifetime
lm.v1localStorage Your trucks, drivers, customers, booked loads and thresholds in the fleet console Until you clear it

That is the complete list — one key, in your own browser. You can verify it: open the developer tools on any page of this site, look under Application → Storage, and count what is there.

None of it is transmitted anywhere. None of it identifies you across sites. There is no advertising identifier, because there is no advertising. There are no cookies at all — not one, not even a functional one.

3. Why none of it needs a consent banner

The ePrivacy Directive — and Serbian law following it — requires consent before storing information on a user's device, except where the storage is strictly necessary to provide a service the user has explicitly requested.

Every item in the table above exists solely to make a feature you asked for work:

  • lm.v1 holds the fleet you entered. Without it, the console forgets everything the moment you reload, which is not a product.

None of them profiles you, tracks you between sites, or feeds anything to a third party. So the exemption applies, and a banner asking permission to remember your own trucks would be compliance theatre rather than privacy.

If analytics are ever added, this page changes first and a real consent mechanism appears with it — one that works before you choose, not one that loads the tracker and then asks.

4. The one third-party request

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. That is a request from your browser to a Google server, and Google therefore sees your IP address and browser details. No cookie is set by that request, but it is a data transfer and German courts have treated it as one requiring a legal basis.

We are disclosing it rather than ignoring it, and it is on the list to remove: the fonts can be served from this site instead, at which point no request to Google happens at all. When that ships, this section says so.

The full picture of who receives what is in the privacy policy.

5. How to clear it

Everything listed in section 2 lives in your own browser and you can delete it without asking us:

  • In the app: Fleet & Data → Erase everything. Export a backup first — we have no copy and cannot restore it.
  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → See all site data and permissions → find this site → Delete.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.

Browser privacy modes and "block all site data" settings will also stop the console from remembering anything. The application handles that without breaking; it simply will not persist.

6. Changes

If storage changes, this page changes with it and the version and date at the top move. We will not quietly add a tracker and leave this page as it is.
