Fuel & MPG Tracker

Turns fill-up receipts into real MPG and real fuel cost per mile.

One row per fill-up: date, truck, driver, odometer, gallons, price. Miles driven, MPG, fuel cost and fuel cost per mile calculate themselves from the odometer gap — you never type a derived number. The dashboard breaks the fleet down by truck and by driver, and a rolling five-fill average smooths out the single short run that makes one week look terrible.

Who it is for. Anyone who has wondered whether a bad fuel month was the price or the truck.

Half an MPG on 120,000 miles at $3.85 diesel is about $4,600 a year. The simulator sheet shows your own number.

HOS Compliance Tracker

All four hours-of-service clocks, checked daily, flagged the day they break.

One row per driver per day. The sheet checks the 11-hour driving limit, the 14-hour on-duty window, the 30-minute break requirement and the rolling 70-hour cycle — computing the cycle by summing the previous eight days for that specific driver automatically, which is the part people get wrong by hand. Violations turn red immediately.

Who it is for. Carriers who would rather find a violation on a Tuesday than at an audit.

The sample data is deliberately realistic: 91% compliance, 17 violations across all four types. Not a clean demo file.

Before you buy

The questions people actually ask

Do I need Excel?

They are .xlsx files with standard formulas — no macros, no add-ins, no VBA. They open in Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice Calc and Google Sheets. A handful of the conditional-formatting colours render slightly differently in Google Sheets; every calculation is identical.

Is this a subscription?

No. There is no recurring charge, nothing renews, and there is no account. You download the file and it is yours. You will still be able to open it in ten years without paying anybody, including us.

Can I change the formulas?

Yes, and you should. Nothing is locked or password-protected. Every cost assumption sits on a visible settings or cost-base sheet precisely so you can replace our numbers with yours.

Can I use it for my whole fleet?

Yes — one purchase covers your own business, however many trucks. What the licence does not allow is reselling or redistributing the file itself. The licence terms say it in one paragraph.

What if it does not help?

Ask for the money back within 60 days, for any reason, and you are not asked to delete your copy. EU and UK consumers also have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal on top of that. See the refund policy.

Is the sample data real?

It is synthetic, and it is deliberately realistic rather than flattering — the HOS file ships at 91% compliance with 17 violations, because a file that shows everything green teaches you nothing about what a violation looks like.

How do I get the file?

Instant download after payment on Gumroad, which takes the payment, applies any VAT or sales tax due in your country at checkout, and issues the receipt. This site has no checkout and never sees your card. Nothing ships by post and nothing waits for approval.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. A workbook is a file on your computer. Nothing in it connects to us, phones home, or requires an internet connection to work.
