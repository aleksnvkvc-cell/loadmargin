Outreach desk

Private page, not linked anywhere. It finds leads and writes the emails; you read each one and press send. Everything you mark is saved only in this browser.

Rules that keep your accounts alive
  • Max 20 cold emails a day from Gmail. More gets you flagged as spam.
  • Every email has an opt-out line and your postal address (U.S. CAN-SPAM law). Fill in the address below once.
  • No LinkedIn or Facebook bots: automation there gets accounts banned. This tool only prepares messages for those.
  • Anyone who says "stop": mark them Do not contact. They never show again.

1. Find leads

Public FMCSA Company Census data (data.transportation.gov): active interstate carriers. Only rows with an email address are useful for email; phone-only rows are kept for calls.

Export from FMCSA, a load board, or any list. Needs columns for name and email; company, state and phone are optional.

Edits are saved. The opt-out line and address are added automatically at the bottom.
