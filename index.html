Method, not magic

Exactly how the number is built

No black box. If a carrier's controller asks how the margin was derived, this is the answer — and it is the same formula running in the calculator above.

total_miles = loaded + deadhead
fuel = (total_miles / mpg) × diesel
driver = miles × cpm | or rate × %
maintenance = total_miles × maint_cpm
fixed = total_miles × fixed_cpm
net = rate − (fuel + driver + maint + fixed + fees)
margin = net / rate

Build your cost base once

Monthly overhead ÷ monthly miles gives your fixed cost per mile. Every carrier's number is different — that difference is exactly why generic rate-per-mile advice fails.

Score the offer against it

Deadhead is counted as real miles, because it burns real fuel. Driver pay is modelled the way you actually pay — per mile or as a percentage of the line haul.

Get a decision, not a dashboard

Accept, negotiate, or walk — with the counter-offer already calculated. Your dispatcher does not need to interpret a chart while a broker is on the line.
