How to calculate your true cost per mile

Most owner-operators underestimate their real cost per mile because they only count fuel. Your true number includes truck payment, insurance, maintenance, tires, permits, tolls, and your own pay.

Add every fixed and variable cost for a month, divide by the miles you ran, and you get your break-even per mile. Any load below that number loses you money - even if the total looks big.

LoadMargin's calculators do this automatically so you can decide in seconds whether a load is worth taking.
