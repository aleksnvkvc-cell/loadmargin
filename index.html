The core idea

A rate is not a margin.

A $2.50 RPM load isn't automatically better than a $2.20 RPM load.

Total miles, deadhead, fuel, driver pay, maintenance and fixed costs decide what a load actually contributes to the business. LoadMargin helps you see the difference before you commit the truck.

Revenue, all of it

Line haul plus accessorials, so you are comparing what the load really pays.

Miles that count

Loaded miles and deadhead together. The empty miles cost the same fuel and wear.

Your real costs

Fuel, driver pay, maintenance, tires, insurance and fixed costs, from your operation, not an average.

What's left

Profit, margin and the break-even rate, so you know what to take, what to counter and what to pass on.

Who you're dealing with

Built around U.S. trucking operations.

LoadMargin is run by one person: a U.S.-trucking-focused support professional based in Serbia, with experience in load support, track & trace and customer communication.

The work is handled personally, not passed to a sales team or an outsourced support desk. The free calculators and guides on this site show how I think about a load. You can check the method before you pay for anything.

Where is LoadMargin based?

LoadMargin is operated remotely from Serbia and focuses specifically on U.S. trucking operations. Communication and services are handled online, by email.

How do I pay?

There is no checkout on this site. Once we agree what you need, you get an invoice by email (PayPal or Payoneer), and the report is sent once it's paid.

What do I need to send?
