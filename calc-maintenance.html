How the truck maintenance cost calculator works

Formula & FAQ +

Truck maintenance arrives in lumps: quiet months, then a large repair bill. This maintenance cost per mile calculator spreads twelve months of spending over the miles you drove, so you can charge a steady reserve to every load instead of being surprised by the next repair.

Enter what you spent on preventive maintenance, tires, brakes, aftertreatment, engine and the rest. It shows your maintenance cost per mile, the monthly amount to set aside, and a separate reserve per mile for a future major overhaul.

maintenance cost per mile = 12-month maintenance spend ÷ miles driven
monthly reserve = 12-month spend ÷ 12
overhaul reserve per mile = expected overhaul cost ÷ miles until overhaul

What is a normal truck maintenance cost per mile?

For many owner-operators it lands somewhere around 15 to 25 cents a mile, depending on the truck's age and how hard it runs. Your own records matter more than any average.

Should tires be included?

Yes. Tires are one of the largest maintenance costs and belong in the per-mile figure.
