The short version

This site has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. The calculators run in your browser and your freight figures never leave it.

The only personal data that reaches anyone here is what you type into the contact form, which arrives as an email. If you buy a workbook, that purchase happens on Gumroad and Gumroad — not this site — handles your payment details.

This policy is drafted against the Serbian Zakon o zaštiti podataka o ličnosti ("ZZPL") and the EU GDPR, which it closely mirrors. It is written to be accurate about what this specific site does, but its author is not a lawyer and it is not legal advice.

Under ZZPL Article 56 and GDPR Article 37 one is required only where processing is large-scale, systematic monitoring, or involves special categories of data at scale. None of those apply to this product. If that changes, this section changes with it and the DPO's contact details appear here.

2. What we collect, and what we deliberately do not

2.1 If you only use the site

The public site is a set of static files served by GitHub Pages. We do not run analytics, we do not set advertising cookies, and we do not fingerprint visitors. GitHub, as the host, records standard server logs including IP addresses for security and abuse prevention — that is GitHub's processing under GitHub's own privacy statement, and we do not receive those logs.

2.2 If you use the contact form

The form collects exactly what it shows on screen:

  • Name — so a reply is addressed to a person
  • Work email — so a reply can be sent
  • Company name — so it is clear which carrier is asking
  • Fleet size band — so the reply is relevant to your scale
  • An optional free-text note — whatever you choose to write

The form is delivered by FormSubmit, which passes the submission to a personal inbox. It is a processor for that step. The form does not ask for a phone number, a postal address, a payment card, a date of birth, or anything about your customers or drivers, and it does not sign you up to anything — there is no mailing list.

2.3 If you buy a workbook

This site takes no payments and has no checkout. Purchases happen on Gumroad — see the terms. Gumroad collects your payment and billing details directly on its own site, under its own privacy policy, and is the controller for those details. What comes back here is a payout report identifying the order and the product — never your card number.

There are no subscriptions, so there is no recurring billing data of any kind.

2.4 What we do not collect at all

  • No card numbers, bank details or payment credentials ever reach this site.
  • No special categories of data (health, biometrics, political opinions, union membership).
  • No location tracking, no device fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking.
  • No freight rates, lane data, customer names or driver records. Those stay in your browser — see section 4.

3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

ZZPL Article 12 and GDPR Article 6 require a lawful basis for every purpose. Ours:

WhatWhyLawful basis
Contact form submissionTo read your question and reply to it Legitimate interest in responding to someone who contacted us — ZZPL Art. 12(1)(6), GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)
Replying to an email you sendTo answer you Legitimate interest in responding to correspondence — ZZPL Art. 12(1)(6), GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)
The order record for a workbook you boughtTo deliver support for what you paid for, and to handle a refund request Performance of a contract — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)

That is the whole table. There is no marketing list, no consent-based processing, no profiling and no analytics, so no lawful basis is claimed for any of them.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling in the sense of GDPR Article 22. The load-scoring engine makes decisions about freight, not about people.

Under Serbian Zakon o elektronskoj trgovini Article 8, a commercial email may only be sent with prior consent. There is no mailing list here, so nobody is added to one.

4. The calculator and the console: nothing leaves your browser

This is the part most privacy policies would bury, so it goes near the top.

The free calculator on the homepage and the fleet console both run entirely in your browser. The rates you enter, the lanes you run, your cost per mile, your customers and your drivers are processed by JavaScript on your own device. Nothing is transmitted to us, because there is no server to transmit it to.

You do not have to take our word for it. Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and use the calculator. You will see no outbound request carrying your figures. That takes about thirty seconds and it is a better guarantee than any sentence we could write.

The console stores your fleet data in localStorage — a per-site storage area inside your own browser. Consequences worth knowing:

  • It never reaches us, and we cannot recover it for you.
  • Clearing your browser's site data erases it. Export a backup from Fleet & Data.
  • Anyone with access to that browser profile can read it. On a shared dispatch computer, that matters.

Because this storage is strictly necessary for a feature you asked for and holds no identifier used to track you, it does not require cookie consent. See the cookie page for the full reasoning.

On the web fonts, plainly: the site loads typefaces from Google's servers, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google sees your IP address. German courts have held this to be a personal data transfer requiring a basis. It is avoidable — the fonts can be served from this site instead, and no request to Google is made at all. Until that change ships, this is disclosed here rather than glossed over.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not build a benchmarking product out of customer data, and we do not aggregate freight or rate information across carriers. Rate and cost information is commercially sensitive, and treating it as an asset would put us in conflict with the people paying for the tool.

6. Transfers outside Serbia and the EEA

GitHub, FormSubmit and Google are established in the United States. Using them means personal data described above is processed there.

Under ZZPL Articles 64–70 and GDPR Chapter V, such a transfer needs a basis. For the United States we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and on Standard Contractual Clauses where it is not. Serbia is on the Council of Europe Convention 108+ list, and the Serbian Commissioner recognises transfers to countries covered by an EU adequacy decision.

You can ask us which mechanism applies to a specific recipient and we will tell you.

8. Your rights, and how to use them

Under ZZPL Articles 26–39 and the equivalent GDPR articles you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what we hold about you and get a copy (ZZPL Art. 26)
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 29)
  • Erasure — have data deleted where there is no reason to keep it (Art. 30)
  • Restriction — have processing paused while a dispute is resolved (Art. 31)
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (Art. 36)
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 37)
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting what was lawful before
  • Not be subject to automated decisions producing legal effects (Art. 38)

To use any of them, email aleksnvkvc@gmail.com and say which right you are exercising. We do not require a form or a specific wording.

We will respond within 30 days, as ZZPL Article 21 requires. Where a request is complex we may extend that by up to 60 days, and we will tell you why within the first 30. There is no charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or repetitive.

We may ask you to confirm your identity — not to obstruct you, but because handing your data to somebody who merely claims to be you would be the larger failure.

8.1 If you are in California

The CCPA and CPRA apply to businesses meeting one of three thresholds: annual gross revenue above roughly USD 26.6 million, the personal information of 100,000 or more California residents, or 50% or more of revenue derived from selling or sharing personal information. We meet none of them, so the CCPA does not currently apply to us.

We are telling you that rather than pasting in a CCPA section that would imply otherwise. The rights in section 8 are available to you regardless of where you live, and we honour them the same way. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it, so a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link would have nothing to switch off.

10. How the data is protected

  • The site and the application are served over HTTPS only.
  • There are no accounts and no passwords, so there is no credential store to breach. Nothing here can be logged into.
  • There is no database and no application server. The site is static files.
  • Access to the inbox receiving contact form messages is protected by two-factor authentication.
  • Payment data is never handled here at all — it is taken by Gumroad, on Gumroad's site.
  • The strongest control is architectural: the freight data never arrives. Data we do not hold cannot be breached.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the Commissioner within 72 hours as ZZPL Article 52 requires, and notify you directly where the risk is high.

11. Children

This is a commercial tool for freight carriers. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 15 — the age of digital consent under ZZPL Article 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the version number and date at the top of the page change with it. If a change materially affects how we handle data you have already given us, we will email you before it takes effect rather than relying on you to notice.

We do not treat continued use of the site as agreement to a new privacy policy where the law requires consent.
