Privacy Policy

1. The short version

  • The calculators send nothing. Everything you type into them stays in your browser.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising or tracking code. The fonts are served from this site.
  • If you email or use a service, the information you send is used only to answer you or do the work, and is never sold or shared for marketing.

Controller: Aleksandar Novaković, an individual, Smederevo, Republic of Serbia. Email: aleksnvkvc@gmail.com.

2. The free tools

The calculators and console run entirely in your browser. Some remember your last inputs in your browser's own storage so they're there next time; that data never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time. Open your browser's network tab while you use them and you'll see no outbound request carrying your figures.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical request logs (such as IP address and browser) when you load a page.

3. When you email

If you email aleksnvkvc@gmail.com, your message, name and email address are received in a Gmail inbox and used to reply to you.

4. When you use a service

For a service you'll send business information: load details, rates, mileage and operating costs, and for compliance services, details of your authority and records. If you send driver documents for a compliance review, they may include personal data about your drivers; send only what is needed and you are responsible for being allowed to share it.

This information is used only to do the work you asked for. It is not shared, sold, pooled with other carriers' data or used to build any benchmark or product. Invoices and payments are handled through PayPal or Payoneer.

7. Transfers outside Serbia

GitHub and Google are established in the United States, and payment providers may process data outside Serbia. For the United States the basis is the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and Standard Contractual Clauses where it is not, consistent with ZZPL Articles 64–70 and GDPR Chapter V.

9. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, or object to processing. Email aleksnvkvc@gmail.com; no form or special wording is needed, and you'll get an answer within 30 days. We don't sell or share personal information as US state privacy laws define it.

10. Contact and complaints

Questions or complaints: aleksnvkvc@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Serbian supervisory authority, the Poverenik za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti (Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, Belgrade), or to the authority in your own country.
