How the per diem calculator works

Formula & FAQ +

Per diem is a flat daily allowance for meals and incidental costs on days you are away from home overnight. Instead of saving meal receipts, an owner-operator can deduct the IRS special transportation rate for each day away: $80 a day inside the U.S. and $86 outside, unchanged for the year starting 1 October 2026 (IRS Notice 2026-60).

Days you leave or come home count at 75% of the rate. Drivers subject to DOT hours-of-service rules deduct 80% of the total, instead of the 50% most businesses get for meals. The tax saved depends on your own rate; 25 to 35% is a common combined range for self-employment and income tax.

per diem = full days × daily rate + partial days × daily rate × 75%
deduction = per diem × 80%
tax saved ≈ deduction × your tax rate

Can company drivers claim per diem?

Generally not as a deduction. Company drivers can only receive it as a non-taxable part of pay if the carrier runs a per diem pay plan.

What counts as a partial day?

The day you leave home and the day you come back. They are counted at 75% of the daily rate.
