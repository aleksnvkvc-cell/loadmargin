Questions

Where is LoadMargin based?

LoadMargin is operated remotely from Serbia and focuses specifically on U.S. trucking operations. Communication and services are handled online, by email.

How do I pay?

There is no checkout on this site. Once we agree what you need, you get an invoice by email, and the payment method is agreed before any work starts. See the terms.

What do I need to send for an audit?

The load details (lanes, rate, accessorials, loaded and deadhead miles) and your operating costs. If you don't have your cost per mile worked out yet, that's fine: I'll tell you exactly what to pull together.

Is the audit a guarantee the load will make money?

No. It's an analysis of the numbers you provide: what the load costs to run and what it should leave you. The decision to take the load stays yours.

Do you sell software or a subscription?

No. The calculators are free, the Excel workbooks are one-time purchases on Gumroad, and services are agreed one at a time by email.
