Questions

Where is LoadMargin based?

LoadMargin is operated remotely from Serbia and focuses specifically on U.S. trucking operations. Communication and services are handled online, by email.

How do I pay?

There is no checkout on this site. Once we agree what you need, you get an invoice by email (PayPal or Payoneer), and the report is sent once it's paid. See the terms.

What do I need to send?

For a Load Check: the load details (lanes, rate, accessorials, loaded and deadhead miles) and your basic operating costs. For the full audit: your monthly costs and the loads or lanes you want priced. If you don't have your cost per mile worked out yet, that's fine: I'll tell you exactly what to pull together.

What's the difference between the $49 and $249 options?

The $49 Load Check answers one question: does this load work with the costs you give me? The $249 audit first builds your real cost per mile from your monthly costs, then prices up to three of your loads or lanes against it, so you know your walk-away rate going forward.

Is this a guarantee the load will make money?

No. It's an analysis of the numbers you provide: what the load costs to run and what it should leave you. The decision to take the load stays yours.

Do you sell software or a subscription?

No. LoadMargin sells services only. The calculators and guides are free, and each service is agreed one at a time by email.
