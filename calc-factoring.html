How the freight factoring calculator works

Formula & FAQ +

Freight factoring gets you paid on an invoice in a day or two instead of 30 to 45 days, for a fee. This factoring calculator turns that fee into what it really costs you: dollars per month, an effective rate on the money you factor, and an annual percentage rate for getting paid early.

It adds the percentage fee, any per-invoice fees and, if your contract has a monthly minimum, only the top-up you pay when your fees fall short of it. The annual rate compares the fee with the number of days you get your money sooner.

monthly cost = (invoice × fee %) × invoices + per-invoice fees + top-up to the monthly minimum
effective rate = monthly cost ÷ invoice volume
annual rate = effective rate ÷ (1 − effective rate) × 365 ÷ days paid sooner

Is 3% factoring expensive?

3% on a 30-day payer works out to roughly a 39% annual rate. It can still be worth it if cash is what stops you from running loads.

What is the difference between factoring and quick pay?

Quick pay is the broker paying early for a fee on one load. Factoring is an ongoing contract with a factoring company for all your invoices.
