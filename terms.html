Who you are dealing with

LoadMargin is run by one person, Aleksandar Novaković, an individual in Smederevo, Serbia, not a registered company. Details are on the legal information page.

1. The short version

  • The calculators, console and guides are free. No account, no card, no limits.
  • LoadMargin sells services only: no files, no software and no subscriptions are sold on this site.
  • Every service is agreed by email (what is included, the price and the timing) before any work starts, and invoiced. There is no checkout here.
  • An analysis is an estimate based on the figures you provide. The decision to take a load is always yours.

2. The free tools

The load calculator, the other calculators, the fleet console and the guides are provided free, as they are. They run in your browser and send nothing anywhere. Using them does not create a contract or any obligation on either side.

Please don't use them to break the law, to attack or overload the site, or to republish the guides as your own. Linking to them and quoting them with a link is welcome.

3. Paid services

The services and their current prices are listed on the Services page: the Quick Load Check, the Full Cost & Lane Audit, the Authority Checklist Review, the New Authority Setup, the Compliance Tune-up, the Load Check Plan and Ongoing Support.

A service is agreed between you and Aleksandar Novaković by email. Before any work starts you will have, in writing: what is included, the price, and when you can expect it. If something on the Services page and the email we agree differ, the email is what counts.

Monthly services (the Load Check Plan and Ongoing Support) are invoiced month by month. You can stop at any time; nothing renews automatically and nothing is charged without an invoice you have seen.

4. Payment and delivery

  • Payment is by invoice, through PayPal or Payoneer. This site never takes or sees card details.
  • Reports are delivered by email, normally as a PDF, after the invoice is paid.
  • If something can't be delivered as agreed, you'll be told straight away and the payment is returned. See the refund policy.

5. What an analysis is and is not

An analysis applies arithmetic to the figures you supply and to any assumptions that are listed in the report. It is only as accurate as those figures. It is not a guarantee of profit, and it is not tax, legal, insurance or financial advice. Compliance services point out what the rules require and what appears to be missing; they don't replace your own responsibility as a carrier, or advice from an attorney or accountant where you need one.

6. Your information

Load, rate and cost figures you send are used only to do the work you asked for. They are not shared, sold or pooled with anyone else's. The privacy policy explains what is kept and for how long.

8. Liability

To the extent the law allows, liability for any paid service is limited to the amount you paid for that service. Nothing here limits liability that cannot legally be limited, or any rights you have as a consumer.

9. Law and disputes
