6. Accuracy, and what this software is not

Read this section even if you skip the rest.

LoadMargin performs arithmetic on numbers you supply. Its output is only as accurate as your inputs. If your cost per mile is wrong, every margin the tool produces is wrong by the same amount, confidently and consistently. The formula is published on the methodology page precisely so you can check it.

Specifically, this software is not:

  • An electronic logging device. The hours-of-service planner is a planning check. It does not record duty status and is not a compliance record. It does not model adverse driving conditions, the short-haul exception, the sleeper-berth split or the 34-hour restart. Your ELD remains your record of duty status.
  • Accounting software. It does not produce financial statements, file returns, or replace a bookkeeper.
  • Legal, tax or financial advice. A margin figure is not advice about whether to take a load, hire a driver or buy a truck. Those remain your commercial decisions.
  • A market forecast. It prices the load in front of you against the costs you carry. It does not predict rates.

We test the cost model seriously. The same arithmetic exists in four independent implementations — the backend, the browser, the spreadsheets and the database views — and they are checked against each other and agree to the cent. That is a real quality control, and it is still not a guarantee that a number is right for your business.
